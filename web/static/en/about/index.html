ABOUT THE PROJECT

How it all works

The details that didn't fit on the home page: how the bot matches you with someone, what happens to your messages, and what Premium is actually for.

MATCHING

How search works

When you tap "Start search," the bot puts you in a shared queue and looks for a free partner who's also searching right now. Pairs form randomly -- no profiles, no interest-matching, just who's available right now.

QUEUE

Who gets matched faster

  1. Free search.A regular queue -- you're matched as free partners become available.
  2. Premium and "Instant search."Priority in the same queue -- these users get matched before everyone else.
  3. Gender/age filters.Premium only. Without it, the bot matches you randomly from everyone searching.
CHAT CONTENT

What happens to your messages

Messages aren't stored in the bot's database -- they're relayed directly through Telegram, like regular messaging. If you file a report, a moderator can temporarily see the last 8 messages of the conversation for 30 minutes -- just enough to review the situation -- then it's deleted automatically.

CONTACT REVEAL

You decide what to show

Your username, phone number and profile photo are visible only to you until both sides of a conversation agree to reveal them. Consent is a separate, explicit action in the bot -- not a side effect of chatting -- your partner can't see your details by accident.

PREMIUM

What the subscription actually gives you

Free, without Premium: search, text chat and photos. Premium adds four specific things -- no promises for features the bot doesn't have.

Filters

Partner's gender and age -- more precise than random matching

Queue priority

Matched faster than the free search

Instant "Next"

Switch partners instantly, no cooldown between chats

Up to 3 profile photos

Instead of the one free photo

AGE REQUIREMENT

18+

Signing up is only available from age 18. If your stated age is outside the allowed range, the bot won't let you past registration.
