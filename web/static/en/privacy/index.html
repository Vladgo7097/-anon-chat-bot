Safety and your data

What a chat partner sees, what the bot processes, and how to delete your account.

Your identity stays private

Your Telegram ID, username and name are not shown automatically. A username and profile photo are shared only after both people use the built-in mutual reveal.

Messages and reports

Messages are relayed through Telegram and are not stored in the bot database. The latest eight text messages are held for up to 30 minutes only to review a report.

Delete your account

Use My profile → Delete account in the bot. Use /help for privacy questions.
